Implementation decision

Reduced-warmth Organic by default. Two honest alternatives.

Settings offers Organic, Utility, and Cool Organic. The selection changes one shared semantic palette and reloads the interface coherently. Nutrition stays overview-first, with evidence on tap and no score layered over incomplete data.

  • Organic retains its colour family, with less yellow in the field.
  • Utility and Cool Organic remain complete selectable palettes.
  • Layout and behavior never fork by theme.
  • Unknown nutrition remains explicitly unknown.
  • Overview first; contributors and detail on tap.
