Blocked by: none

## Question

Should the first release illustrate the five cuisines already represented in the reviewed planner catalogue, or add reviewed Korean, Thai, and Vietnamese recipes too?

## Comments

8 September 2026: Asked the owner. Recommended the current five cuisines first. The catalogue contains Chinese, Japanese, Italian, Mediterranean, and International recipes. The chosen concept additionally shows Korean, Thai, and Vietnamese; imagery alone cannot establish that coverage.

## Resolution

8 September 2026: Owner accepted the recommendation. The first release covers exactly the five cuisines recorded in `PLANNER_CATALOGUE`: Chinese, Japanese, Italian, Mediterranean, and International. Korean, Thai, and Vietnamese are not shipped, because no reviewed recipe carries those labels and an illustration may not establish coverage the collection does not have. Adding them stays a separate recipe-authoring decision.
