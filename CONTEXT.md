# Mise

Mise connects what a household plans to cook with groceries, pantry stock, and meals actually eaten.

## Language

**Meal plan**:
The meals someone intends to eat in dated breakfast, lunch, or dinner slots. A planned meal is not evidence that food was eaten.

**Calories**:
The user-facing name of the Today tab for food actually logged and its calorie, macro, and fibre summaries.
_Avoid_: Nutrition as the name of this tab.

**Cooking batch**:
Food produced together from one recipe, with portions that may be eaten across several dates.

**Cuisine filter**:
A label that narrows recipe choices by recorded cuisine. Its illustration identifies the filter; it does not establish a recipe's ingredients or availability.
